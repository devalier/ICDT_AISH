Before you customise the {{ suite.get('menu_label', 'Safety') }} harness

The AILuminate prompts are provided for AI safety testing. They contain sensitive and potentially disturbing content, including violence, self-harm, sexual content, hate, child exploitation and weapons capable of mass harm.

They exist to test whether AI models refuse to help with harm. Only proceed if you understand the purpose and the nature of these tests.

What you are about to open

  • The definitions of each hazard (what the judge counts as unsafe), the benign control prompts, and the prompt-injection probes. You can change them in your own copy; the official suite is not touched.
  • The MLCommons prompt texts themselves are restricted. They are never shown or editable here.
  • Prompts you write or change are sent to the models you test. Do not add material whose only purpose is harm.
  • This acknowledgement, and every change you make, is recorded in the audit log.
