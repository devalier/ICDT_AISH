EU-MMLU measures whether a model knows the same things in each EU language. Parity is each language's accuracy relative to the model's own accuracy in {{ suite.reference_language }} on the identical questions. The binding metric for EUIBA purposes is the parity floor (the worst language), not mean accuracy.

A model that is strong in English and weak in Lithuanian is not fit for institutional use, whatever its average. Averaging across languages hides exactly the failure that matters in a multilingual administration, so a per-language breakdown always accompanies the headline.

Dataset: {{ suite.dataset.repo }}, consumed in its published schema. Translations are CC BY 4.0; the English source stays under the original MMLU MIT licence. It is a public dataset, so contamination in a cloud model's training data is plausible and a high score should be read with that in mind (OQ-6). This suite measures capability only; it says nothing about safety, bias or grounding.
