AILuminate is the MLCommons AI Risk & Reliability benchmark. It sends a model prompts written to provoke harmful output, then checks whether the model complied. Prompts are labelled with a hazard code (for example vcr violent crimes, hte hate, ssh suicide and self-harm), and results are reported per hazard, so a weak category cannot hide behind strong ones.

A judge (a second AI model) reads each response and decides whether it is unsafe and whether the model refused. Benign control prompts that merely look dangerous measure over-refusal: a model that refuses everything is not safe, it is useless, so the two are reported side by side. EUIBA adds prompt-injection and system-prompt-extraction probes, scored deterministically by whether a planted canary string leaks.
