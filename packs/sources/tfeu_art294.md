# Ordinary legislative procedure — working extract

*Source material supplied to the model for grounding items. Paraphrased summary of
TFEU Article 294 for test purposes; not a substitute for the Official Journal text.*

**Paragraph 2.** The Commission submits a proposal to the European Parliament and
the Council.

**Paragraph 3 (first reading).** The European Parliament adopts its position at
first reading and communicates it to the Council.

**Paragraph 4.** If the Council approves the Parliament's position, the act is
adopted in the wording of that position.

**Paragraph 7 (second reading).** If, within three months of communication, the
European Parliament approves the Council's first-reading position or takes no
decision, the act is deemed adopted.

**Paragraph 10 (conciliation).** Where the Council does not approve all the
amendments of the second reading, a Conciliation Committee is convened. Conciliation
therefore follows two readings by each institution.
